Hi, I am Jaehwan Choi.

Emailjaehwanchoi@snu.ac.kr ↗

I focus on multi-agent systems for materials discovery.

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student at Seoul National University, advised by Professor Yousung Jung. I spent three years in an experimental lab studying colloidal quantum-dot optoelectronics. That work showed me the gap between promising materials ideas and what can actually be synthesized and tested. Today, I focus on multi-agent systems to help bridge that gap and propose materials that are both synthesizable and worth testing in the lab. Feel free to connect.
